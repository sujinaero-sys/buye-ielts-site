The Economics of Attention in the Digital Age

As digital platforms compete for a limited resource, human attention, economists have begun applying traditional market concepts such as scarcity and value to describe how technology companies design products. The average person is estimated to check their phone roughly 80 times per day. One study measured a 25 percent drop in sustained reading among frequent app users. The term 'attention economy' was popularised in the 1990s. Some platforms now report daily active users to investors every three months. A design feature called infinite scroll was introduced to reduce natural stopping points.
