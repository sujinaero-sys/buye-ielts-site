The Philosophy and Practice of Minimalism in Design

Minimalism, an approach that favours simplicity and the deliberate removal of unnecessary elements, has influenced fields ranging from architecture to product design over the past century. The phrase 'less is more' is often associated with a modernist architect from the twentieth century. Minimalist product design often removes visible buttons in favour of clean surfaces. A study of consumer preferences found that 63 percent of participants associated minimalist packaging with higher quality. Critics argue minimalist design can sometimes reduce usability for users unfamiliar with a product. Minimalist architecture frequently relies on open floor plans.
