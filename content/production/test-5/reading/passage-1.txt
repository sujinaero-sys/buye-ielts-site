The Domestication of the Horse

The domestication of the horse marked a turning point in human history, transforming transport, agriculture and warfare across many societies over thousands of years. Genetic evidence suggests horses were first domesticated on the Eurasian steppe. Early domestication is estimated to have occurred around 5500 years ago. Horses were initially kept mainly for their meat and milk. The invention of the bridle allowed riders far greater control. Mounted warfare is believed to have spread rapidly from around 1000 BCE.
