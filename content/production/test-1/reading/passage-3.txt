Automation and the Future of Craft Professions

As robotics and machine learning extend into fields once considered safe from automation, commentators increasingly ask whether skilled manual trades and craft professions will endure the next wave of technological change. A recent survey found that 42 percent of master furniture makers reported using computer-guided tools for at least part of their work. The report estimates that fully automated systems can currently replicate around 70 percent of the physical motions used in traditional joinery. Craft apprenticeships in the surveyed region declined by 15 percent over the past decade. One workshop owner interviewed had trained apprentices for over 30 years.
