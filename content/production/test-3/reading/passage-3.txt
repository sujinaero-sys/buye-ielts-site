The Neuroscience of Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

Understanding how the human brain makes decisions when outcomes are uncertain has become a major focus of cognitive neuroscience, with implications ranging from financial planning to medical diagnosis. Researchers commonly use a card-based gambling task involving four decks. Brain imaging studies frequently highlight activity in the prefrontal cortex during risky choices. One study recorded decision times across 100 trials per participant. Participants with damage to this brain region showed poorer long-term decision-making. The researchers propose that emotional signals help guide decisions through a process called somatic marking.
