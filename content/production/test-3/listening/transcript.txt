Water access is provided from a shared tank. Applications close on the last day of April. New plot holders receive a free bag of compost. The scheme is coordinated by the residents association. Priority for the new plots is given to local residents living within the borough boundary. All plot holders are required to attend a single induction session before receiving their keys. Any plot left unused will be reassigned to another applicant after six months, the coordinator explained. Shared tools are kept in the site office and can be borrowed by any registered plot holder.

[Part 4]
You will hear part of a lecture on the development of written language. First you have some time to look at questions 31 to 40. The earliest known writing system developed in Mesopotamia. Early writing was primarily used for trade records. Cuneiform script was written on tablets made of clay. The lecturer dates the earliest full sentences in writing to around 2600 BCE. Alphabetic writing systems reduced the number of symbols needed to fewer than 30. The spread of alphabets was greatly helped by trade across the Mediterranean Sea. Movable type printing was developed in China during the eleventh century. The Gutenberg press, introduced in Europe, dramatically increased literacy rates. The lecturer notes that digital text has changed reading habits within the last 20 years. The lecture ends by discussing the future role of natural language processing.
