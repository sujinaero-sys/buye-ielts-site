BUYE IELTS - Listening Test 3 staging content

This test will use Question Paper mode:

- The supplied question PNG pages are shown in the exam workspace.
- Students answer using separate numbered answer fields for Questions 1-40.
- The image pages are reference material only; answers are not typed into or edited
  inside the PNG files.
- The answer panel will preserve answers, show answered/unanswered status, and
  support navigation to Questions 1-40.
- The audio will be shown in the Listening panel and connected only to Test 3.
- The test will remain local/staging until the complete test is reviewed.

Recommended files:

- audio.mp3
- questions-01-10.png
- questions-11-20.png
- questions-21-30.png
- questions-31-40.png
- answer-key.png
- transcript.txt (optional)
- support-materials/ (optional maps, diagrams, or images)

Use only content that is user-owned, original, or properly licensed.
Do not upload passwords, private keys, production exports, or confidential student data.
