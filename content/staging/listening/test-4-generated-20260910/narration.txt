IELTS Listening Practice Test 4.

Part 1. You will hear a customer booking a table at the Riverside Restaurant. Read questions 1 to 10.

Good afternoon, Riverside Restaurant. How can I help you?
Hello. I would like to reserve a table for four people, please.
Certainly. Which evening would you like?
Friday evening, if possible.
We have a table at seven thirty p.m. Would that suit you?
Yes, that would be perfect. The booking is under the surname Patel.
Would you like a table in a particular place?
Near the window, please. One of our guests needs a vegetarian meal.
That is no problem. We are at 18 Market Street. Your booking reference is K418.
Thank you. It is also my sister's birthday. Do you provide anything special?
Yes, we provide a free dessert for birthday guests. Please tell us when you arrive.
That is very kind. What should we do if we are delayed?
Please call the restaurant. We can hold the table for fifteen minutes.
That is all I need. Thank you.

Part 2. You will hear a guide describing a new community centre. Read questions 11 to 20.

Welcome to the Riverside Community Centre. The building is beside the river and will open to the public in September. The main hall can hold 250 people. Its roof has been designed as a garden, with native plants and benches.
On the first floor, the largest room is used for dance classes. The cafe is open every day except Mondays. On Saturday mornings, the weekly farmers' market takes place in the courtyard.
Visitors can hire bicycles from the information desk near the main entrance. The car park entrance is on Station Road. Finally, anyone applying for membership must include a recent photograph.

Part 3. You will hear two students discussing an urban gardening project. Read questions 21 to 30.

Maya, why did you choose the urban gardening project?
It matched my career plans. I want to work in environmental planning.
How did the first survey go?
The first problem was that too few people replied. We needed a larger sample.
So what did you change?
We changed the wording of the questionnaire. The original questions were too formal.
The supervisor also advised us to include more information about water use.
That makes sense. What about the interview recordings?
They contain useful quotations, especially from the community volunteers.
What will you do next?
We will analyse the survey results before preparing the final report.
For the related research task, our topic is the effect of trees on city temperatures. One source is a report from the geography department. Another source is interviews with local residents. The final presentation should include a chart and a short video.

Part 4. You will hear a lecture about coastal wetlands. Read questions 31 to 40.

Coastal wetlands are valuable natural systems. One important benefit is that they reduce storm damage by slowing water before it reaches towns.
Many wetlands have disappeared because they were drained for development. Restoration often includes planting mangroves. The main purpose of mangrove planting is to stabilise the shoreline.
Restored wetlands do not always look identical to natural wetlands, but they can recover some important functions. Wetland plants remove excess nutrients from agricultural water and trap particles of sediment.
Restoration must be treated as a long-term project. It needs regular monitoring, and local people should be involved from the planning stage.
One useful example is on the coast of Vietnam. After restoration, the project reduced annual flood damage by 30 percent.
