Academic or General Training — practice the one you need

Question types and topics differ between the two versions of IELTS. BUYE IELTS keeps them separate so your practice matches your actual test.

IELTS Academic

For university and professional registration

For applicants to universities and professional bodies, with academic-style reading passages and a Task 1 that asks you to describe a graph, chart, or diagram.

  • Academic reading passages
  • Graph & diagram Task 1
  • Formal essay topics

IELTS General Training

For work and migration visas

For work, migration, and secondary education applicants, with everyday-context reading passages and a Task 1 letter-writing exercise.

  • Workplace & everyday passages
  • Letter-writing Task 1
  • General essay topics
